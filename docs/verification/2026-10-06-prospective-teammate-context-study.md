# Independent prospective teammate-context study

## Purpose and scope

The October 4 Questionable-only context group won 12/15 across five games.
Its model errors were lower than the matching market's, but its strict
recent-20 control had slightly lower contract-weighted errors. That group was
inspected after results and cannot establish repeatable alpha or a 75% service.
See [the original and exploratory results](2026-10-06-teammate-context-market-comparison.md).

The new `kalshi-nfl-prospective-teammate-context-v1` study tests this one primary
group on **new captures starting October 8**. October 4 is rejected, including
attempts to attach a retrospective prospective receipt. All seven original bins
and the unchanged probability >=75% subset remain secondary diagnostics.
This is an NFL paper-contract study; it does not validate NBA/CFB or ordinary
bookmaker execution. No model adjustment or consumer qualification is added.

## Frozen pregame evidence

The existing publisher first replays the original seven inputs, retained
schedule, source-week, receiving-integrity, source context and recent-gap gates.
It then derives a separate `.local/<capture>/teammate-study.json` from the
**already collected** raw injury/weekly-roster/stat sources. No extra source or
quote requests, tasks or credits are added. Source-unavailable receipts retain
every original candidate with unavailable context.

Only a hash-only `docs/verification/next-nfl-context-<stamp>-anchor.json` enters
the **private** repo. It travels alongside the unchanged primary marker in the
publisher's same amended commit and same preserved research reference. The
original primary marker, commitment and study remain untouched. The study
requires its marker in that exact preserved commit and GitHub's original
server-dated push before kickoff; author time is insufficient. All six sources
bound by the original independent recent-gap evaluator remain unchanged.

The receipt freezes the whole policy, normalized source hashes for the study
and reused scoring/chronology helpers, complete derived candidate membership,
all selected fixtures, source-receipt hash and original commitment digest.
Scoring rebuilds the receipt exactly from the retained sources before accepting
authenticated exact-contract settlement evidence. Later edits to membership,
policy, implementation or source bytes fail replay. Future version changes
need a new protocol/study rather than relabeling these cases.

## Current team, status and chronology

- Club identity comes from the exact frozen venue offer and fixture, joined to
  the retained schedule by both clubs and New York game date. Jacksonville's
  JAX/JAC aliases are normalized. Historical team identity is not a fallback.
- The subject requires one same-week, same-club `ACT` roster row. This denotes
  roster status, **not game-day participation**. Duplicate/cross-club identities,
  missing roster/report coverage and unknown injury positions remain unavailable.
- Missing final designations for reported skill teammates remain unavailable;
  missing reports do not imply a healthy team. Non-skill reports are distinct
  from skill-teammate exposure. The subject's own status is retained separately.
- Acute exposure uses targets from at most the last three scheduled, completed
  same-season club games strictly before the decision's New York date. Missing
  weeks, target values, identities, duplicate rows, nonfinite/negative targets
  or a zero denominator yield unavailable exposure. Future-week stats cannot
  influence the share. Questionable-only exposure needs no acute-target history.
- These are asset snapshots. Individual injury-row update times are unavailable;
  the study makes no live-status or causal teammate-effect claim.

## Window and independent fixture ownership

This **separate study only** admits decisions made >0 and <=48 hours before
kickoff. Thursday's snapshot can own Thursday's fixture but cannot own Sunday's
context ahead of Saturday's fresher scheduled snapshot. Outside-window candidates
remain counted explicitly. The primary next-series ownership rule is unchanged.

Among eligible captures, the earliest **server-published** capture owns the entire
fixture, including fixtures with no candidates. Ownership precedes membership,
price and result filtering. Unknown context, missing prices and nonbinary results
never cause later captures to replace that fixture. All excluded overlaps and
outside-window candidates are counted; retained candidates partition all seven
bins without dropping unavailable rows.

## Scoring and evidence requirement

The study reuses the exact side-oriented matching-price parser, strict recent-20
control, Brier/log-loss scores and deterministic game/player cluster intervals.
Every comparison uses the identical paired outcome/price subset. Empty bins,
missing prices and nonbinary results retain their full-group denominators.
Fewer than two paired games or players suppress cluster intervals.

Primary association support requires all of:

1. At least **50 independent paired games in the Questionable-only group**.
2. Complete result and matching-price coverage for that fixed primary group.
3. Favorable game **and** player upper bounds for both Brier **and** log loss
   against **both** matching market and strict recent-20 probabilities.
4. Replayed pregame sources/membership and verified server-published anchors.

This is an association test. Even support cannot establish a causal context
effect, fills, fees, executable ROI or consumer alpha. Reports always return
`edge_established: false`, `qualified_picks: 0`, `execution_ready: false`,
`fills_verified: false` and `roi: null`. A secondary bin cannot replace the
primary hypothesis after results. Existing six scheduled captures may not
supply 50 primary games; the threshold is not reduced to fit their sample.

## Optional-context failures isolated before the first new capture

The original forecast gates require its quoted club's metadata but do not
guarantee additional opponent-target details. The new study had indexed both
targets directly. A missing opponent could therefore raise `KeyError` and
prevent the shared publisher from anchoring an otherwise valid original capture.
Unsupported injury/roster Parquet columns had the same failure mode. Neither
failure is an authenticated healthy-team report.

The study now retains every original candidate and selected fixture with
`coverage_unavailable` when exact opponent/schedule identity is unavailable.
Authenticated raw context assets whose columns or identity/label types cannot
be interpreted retain `source_status: observed` and separately record
`study_source_status: context_schema_unavailable`. No partial context is treated
as healthy. Changed source bytes, missing/unbounded files, chronology failures,
primary forecast/decision mismatches and settlement proof failures still reject
the capture/report through the original gates. This does not permit a corrupt
primary source to publish.

Unrecognized injury positions also remain unavailable instead of silently
becoming non-skill teammates. Recognized non-skill positions are explicitly
included in the frozen study policy; they use the existing consumer offense/
defense classifications plus special-team positions. Position ambiguity cannot
create the primary Questionable-only group or a no-reported-risk group.
