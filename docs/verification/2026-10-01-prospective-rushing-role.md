# Prospective NFL rushing-role candidate

Version `nfl-rushing-role-shadow-v1` starts after 2026-10-01 05:00 UTC. It is a private, untraded research field on newly recorded NFL rushing forecasts. It does not alter the primary model probability, qualification, exposure, public pick, or existing frozen receiving v2 shadow. Loading the additional source rows uses the existing read-only database query and no provider request.

The candidate requires the pinned NFL/ESPN player crosswalk, a current roster identity, exact quote and line evidence, and complete offensive participation for its corrected three-season history. Source observations and any explicit missing-game supplements must predate the scan cutoff. It must reproduce the original model's rushing sample size, mean and probability before calculating its own estimate. At least two current-season team games, an older prior, a latest game on the captured roster team, and a latest-game gap of no more than 30 days are required. The current-role estimate shrinks current-season hit frequency toward the older-game Jeffreys fraction with a fixed strength of 20. Unknown participation or source conflict abstains.

The compressed source inputs, exact offer and availability hashes, formula version and candidate value are retained with the forecast. Original private paper receipts include this new field in their immutable evidence, binding the candidate to the same original player, side, line and matching market price. The separately frozen receiving shadow and original paper selector remain unchanged.

The fixed `nfl-rushing-individual-pick-audit-v1` rule selects only original raw-model rushing paper picks with both original and recorded rushing-role probabilities at least 75%. Missing candidates are abstentions, never replacement picks. The audit uses authenticated settlements and matched paper prices. Review requires at least 50 decided individual picks across 25 games, a game-cluster win-rate lower bound of 75%, positive flat-stake ROI lower bound, and candidate Brier error below the matched market with a negative game-cluster upper bound. It never promotes automatically. Earlier paper selections have no original rushing-role receipt and cannot enter this prospective candidate cohort.

A favorable early result does not establish a 75% win rate or betting edge. Reports and source inputs stay private. The future audit must run on Linux CPython 3.12 to replay the committed compressed source bytes.
