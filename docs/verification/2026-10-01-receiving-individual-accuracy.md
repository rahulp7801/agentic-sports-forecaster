# Fixed prospective NFL receiving-pick accuracy audit

Protocol `nfl-receiving-individual-pick-audit-v1` starts **2026-10-01 13:00 UTC**. It measures future original raw-model paper selections for receptions and receiving yards. The original immutable receipt fixes the player, game, side, line, book, offered price and market comparator. A receipt and its forecast must both be captured after the protocol starts. An omitted or invalid original/current-role source receipt is an abstention; no later quote, line, market or player replaces it.

The frozen rule requires the original probability, complete-source recent-ten Jeffreys probability and current-role prior-20 probability all to be at least 75%. The latest game must be within 30 days, on the captured roster team, and there must be at least two current-season/current-team games plus older history. The existing verified `nfl-recent-role-shadow-v2` receipt provides these source and timing checks. The first 50 eligible original selections in database receipt order form a closed cohort; later eligible selections are reported but never admitted. Original paper selections that fail the rule remain in coverage counts. The underlying original paper selector and source verifier remain frozen.

Only authenticated settlements at the original offered prices count. The full 50 must settle across at least 25 distinct games. Review requires an observed win rate and game-cluster 95% lower bound at least 75%, a positive lower bound on flat-stake hypothetical ROI, and negative upper bounds on candidate-minus-matching-market and candidate-minus-original Brier error. Reports always retain `promote=false` and `target_verified=false`; a passing report requires independent review, including the effect of trying multiple related strategies. An incomplete cohort cannot be extended after results are seen. This study does not change qualification, dashboard picks, model probabilities, quotas, reserve timing, or place bets.

Run privately on Linux CPython 3.12 with locked dependencies and a read-only export: `python -m sportsbet.quant.nfl_receiving_accuracy_v1 --inputs PRIVATE_INPUTS.json --output PRIVATE_REPORT.json`. Inputs and outputs contain private paper selections and must stay off GitHub. The evaluator pins the existing context verifier and candidate source/artifact hashes. Tests cover sample closure, future capture, missing context, pending settlements, matching-price returns and source failure.
