# Four-slot paid scan timing against the pick lock

The private paid workflow had one all-sport daily run at 12:17 UTC and three all-sport scans at 00:19, 16:19, and 20:19 UTC every day. The consumer pick board freezes a recommendation at 60 minutes before kickoff. Comparing scheduled starts with the retained 2026 Week 4 NFL schedule (games asset SHA-256 `4f27270efeba3af862d250a821ff00578da1ce158b76576b72aa283751b22662`) found a T-120 to T-70 scan opportunity for only the 13:30 UTC game. The 16:19 slot follows the 17:00 group's lock, the 20:19 slot follows the 20:05 group's kickoff, and the 00:19 slot follows the late group's lock. Fourteen of the 15 games lacked a scheduled scan in that prelock window. This counts scheduling opportunities, not quotes or eligible players.

The workflow now retains **four** normal paid opportunities per UTC date. Sunday scan slots are 15:35, 18:45 and 22:45 UTC; Monday keeps 00:19 and 16:19 and moves the late slot to 22:45. The 12:17 daily run remains. On Tuesday through Friday the previous three scan times remain. Saturday's third scan moves from 20:19 to 23:55 UTC. In the pinned Week 4 fixture list, all 15 NFL games now have a scheduled T-120 to T-70 opportunity. The earliest 13:30 game has only 13 minutes between its scheduled daily start and the lock; a delayed or slow job can still miss it.

As a cross-sport check, one ignored, read-only [public ESPN FBS scoreboard response](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/college-football/scoreboard?dates=20261003&groups=80&limit=1000) for October 3 (`dates=20261003&groups=80&limit=1000`, SHA-256 `18d52d9b23e433fcafbfdd134ef7dbdf24231f5e83bcbad978bb75503497228a`) contained 25 events. Its Fresno State at Washington State game started 2026-10-04 01:30 UTC. The original Sunday 00:19 scan was in that game's prelock window; the new Saturday 23:55 scan preserves a prelock opportunity. In that limited response, one of 25 games had a scheduled prelock slot both before and after this change. The 25-event response is not a complete all-CFB coverage proof. Saturday's shifted slot can change coverage for other kickoff times; NBA has no October 4 game and its later season needs a separate live timing audit.

The schedule retains the existing `DATA_PIPELINE_ENABLED` guard, 20-credit daily and 450-credit rolling limits, staged pregame reserve, quote cadence, source checks, model versions and qualification thresholds. It adds no jobs or provider calls at review time. A scheduled opportunity cannot guarantee a run: GitHub can delay scheduled workflows, the $0 account gate currently blocks hosted jobs before steps, and the credit limit allows far fewer than 15 NFL event quote checks in one day. No pick, price, win rate or edge is inferred. Verify first actual protected-master runs, paid-credit accounting and pick-board publication only after CI and deployment are restored.
